KFC Hot and Spicy Wings: The Big Comeback Story

The Kentucky fried hot wings have quite a comeback story. After relentless fan demand, KFC's beloved Potato Wedges and top rated Hot & Spicy Wings officially came back by obsessive demand beginning August 18 at KFCs nationwide.

The wings, which had not been on the menu for nearly two years, feature a spicy marinade and are coated with extra crispy breading. This is the same preparation style that made KFC hot and spicy wings a global fan favorite before they were temporarily removed from U.S. menus.

The wings are spicy marinated and double hand breaded in KFC's signature Extra Crispy breading. The result is a spicy wing with an undeniable crunch appeal.

Why Were KFC Hot Wings Removed?

Many fans searched for "KFC hot wings discontinued" after they disappeared from U.S. menus. The truth is they were never permanently gone. KFC removed them temporarily while focusing on other menu items, but the overwhelming fan response on social media and petitions brought them back.

For years, KFC heard from fans clamoring for a chance to try their famous products again, through thousands of social media comments and petition signatures.

Nutritional Info: How Many Calories in KFC Wings?

Health conscious readers always want the calorie count before ordering. Here is what you should know:

A KFC Hot Wing is usually considered the lighter wing option. Many menu guides place it around the 70 to 80 calorie range per piece.

Wing Type Calories (Per Piece)
Hot Wing ~70 calories
Original Recipe Wing ~120 calories
Extra Crispy Wing ~170 calories

While Hot Wings are the lowest calorie option per piece, a 6 piece serving pack has over 1,100mg of sodium, which is nearly 50% of the recommended daily intake for adults.

So if you are watching sodium, keep portion sizes in check and skip extra sauces.
